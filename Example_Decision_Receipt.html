Assumptions travel with the decision

Continuous linear program maximizes minimum distributional overlap across selected historical county claim mixes. Largest-remainder rounding converts weights to integer appointment slots; scores are recalculated after rounding.

Limits: Eligible insured claim records are not unique households, requests, uninsured losses, population exposure, or future need. Historical scenario overlap does not measure people helped or service quality. Optimizing and evaluating on the same selected scenarios is descriptive stress testing, not predictive validation.

Each slot has equal cost. Travel, staffing, urgency, and minimum service are not modeled. This is a rehearsal, not an operational recommendation.
